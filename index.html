AcceptEdge

A guided writing coach for academic papers. Pick a target journal (ABDC 2022 categorized) and paper type, then write section by section with editor-grade prompts, cite-as-you-type references, and an acceptance-readiness checklist. No account, no server — your manuscript lives in your browser's local storage. Citation lookups use the free Crossref API.
